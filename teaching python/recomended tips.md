## comments

ever forget what a piece of code does, and then you have to do research all over again?

Or do people who look at your code ask you questions about what each line of code does, and you just don't want to explain all of it?

well, thats why comments are a thing! for example:

the computer will completley ignore anything after a <u># , wich means that you can read over it, but the computer can't!

## new line

so you want a new line in your output? well, don't waste your time with 2 prints!

just use \n!

\n is put INSIDE the string, however, it is not shown in the output. for example:

print("we all fell down.\nthat was horrible.")

this will output the below:

we all fell down.

that was horrible.
